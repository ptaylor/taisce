Taisce — privacy policy What this app does with your data

Privacy policy

Effective 31 October 2026. This policy covers the Taisce web app at ptaylor.github.io/taisce and nothing else.

What Taisce is

Taisce is a static web application. There is no Taisce account, no Taisce server and no Taisce database. Your notes are stored in your own Dropbox account and are read and written directly from your browser using the Dropbox API.

What Taisce accesses

  • Only the app folder. The Dropbox app is registered as an App folder app, so it can see and change files in Apps/Taisce in your Dropbox account — never the rest of it.
  • Permissions requested: files.metadata.read (list files and folders), files.content.read (open a note or an image), files.content.write (save a note, create a file or folder, upload a pasted image or the PDF made when sharing) and sharing.read / sharing.write (create or reuse a shared link when you press Share). Sharing is never automatic: no link exists until you ask for one.
  • Shared links are yours. A link you create with Share is a Dropbox shared link for what you chose to share: the Markdown note itself, or a PDF copy generated beside it. It is public: anyone who has the URL can read that file. Taisce does not send it anywhere — it is copied to your clipboard, or handed to your device's share sheet, and nowhere else.
  • Nothing else. Taisce does not request your name, email, contacts, other apps, or any other Dropbox data.

What is stored, and where

  • In your browser: a Dropbox refresh token and a short-lived access token in localStorage, plus preferences (theme, how many recent files to show, how many undo steps to keep, and whether to share as PDF or Markdown). In PDF mode it also keeps a small per-note record of what was shared — the note's path and a fingerprint of its text, never the text itself — so the app can tell you when the PDF needs republishing. No note content is kept in the browser once you leave the page. Browser storage is per origin, and this app is served from ptaylor.github.io, which also hosts other project pages of the same account — those pages share this storage. A separate domain would isolate it.
  • In your Dropbox account: your notes, pictures you paste into a note, and, when you share in PDF mode, the generated PDF copy beside that note.
  • On our servers: nothing. We do not operate a server for this app. There is no advertising and no tracking pixel, and no third-party script is loaded: the Markdown renderer and the analytics counter are both bundled with the app. The only network requests it makes are to the Dropbox API, to GoatCounter (usage counts, described below) and to the files of the app itself.

Usage measurement

Counting how the app is used is the only way to find out which parts are worth keeping, so Taisce sends anonymous counts to GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It is deliberately limited:

  • No cookies are set and nothing is stored on your device for it. GoatCounter does not build a profile or follow you to other sites.
  • What is sent is a count of an event name from a fixed list — "a note was opened", "a link was edited", "the slash menu was used", "a save conflicted", "PDF sharing was chosen" — together with the release of the app that sent it. Never a file name, a folder, a search term, the contents of a note, a shared link, or anything from your Dropbox account.
  • GoatCounter records the page address, referring site, browser, screen width and a salted hash of your IP address, which it discards; the salt changes every day. Your IP address is not stored.
  • The demo is not counted. Opening the app with ?mock=1 sends nothing at all.
  • The counter is the one request that can be blocked for you: a content blocker that stops taisce.goatcounter.com has no effect on the app itself.

What a note cannot do

A note is untrusted input. Opening one never contacts a third party: images that point at other websites are shown as links and are not fetched, styling attributes are removed, scripts are stripped, and a Content-Security-Policy blocks anything that slips through. Images in the note's own _assets folder are the only pictures loaded, and they come from your Dropbox with your token.

How your data is used

Exclusively to do what you asked: list the files in the app folder, open the one you tapped, and save your edits back to it. Your data is never sold, shared, published or used to train anything.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Disconnect in the app asks Dropbox to revoke the app’s access and forgets it on the device. Clearing the site's data in your browser does the same locally.
  • You can also remove the app from Connected apps in your Dropbox account settings.
  • Your notes remain in your Dropbox account until you delete them there. Taisce holds no copy.

Security

The app is served over HTTPS. Authorisation uses OAuth 2 with PKCE, so no client secret exists in the app or in this repository. Everything rendered from a note is sanitised before it reaches the page. The token in localStorage is the deliberate trade for having no backend; that is why the app loads no third-party scripts.

Children

Taisce is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it. Questions or problems: open an issue at github.com/ptaylor/taisce.

How Dropbox handles your data is described in the Dropbox privacy policy.
