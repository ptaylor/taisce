Taisce — privacy policy What this app does with your data

Privacy policy

Effective 31 October 2026. This policy covers the Taisce web app at ptaylor.github.io/taisce and nothing else.

What Taisce is

Taisce is a static web application. There is no Taisce account, no Taisce server and no Taisce database. Your notes are stored in your own Dropbox account and are read and written directly from your browser using the Dropbox API.

What Taisce accesses

  • Only the app folder. The Dropbox app is registered as an App folder app, so it can see and change files in Apps/Taisce in your Dropbox account — never the rest of it.
  • Permissions requested: files.metadata.read (list files and folders), files.content.read (open a note or an image) and files.content.write (save a note, create a file or folder, upload a pasted image).
  • Nothing else. Taisce does not request your name, email, contacts, other apps, or any other Dropbox data.

What is stored, and where

  • In your browser: a Dropbox refresh token and a short-lived access token in localStorage, plus two preferences (how many recent files to show, how many undo steps to keep). No note content is kept in the browser once you leave the page. Browser storage is per origin, and this app is served from ptaylor.github.io, which also hosts other project pages of the same account — those pages share this storage. A separate domain would isolate it.
  • In your Dropbox account: your notes, and pictures you paste into a note, which are uploaded to an assets folder beside that note.
  • On our servers: nothing. We do not operate a server for this app, and there is no analytics, advertising, tracking pixel or third-party script. The Markdown renderer is bundled with the app; the only network requests it makes are to the Dropbox API, plus the files of the app itself.

What a note cannot do

A note is untrusted input. Opening one never contacts a third party: images that point at other websites are shown as links and are not fetched, styling attributes are removed, scripts are stripped, and a Content-Security-Policy blocks anything that slips through. Images in the note's own assets folder are the only pictures loaded, and they come from your Dropbox with your token.

How your data is used

Exclusively to do what you asked: list the files in the app folder, open the one you tapped, and save your edits back to it. Your data is never sold, shared, published or used to train anything.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Disconnect in the app asks Dropbox to revoke the app’s access and forgets it on the device. Clearing the site's data in your browser does the same locally.
  • You can also remove the app from Connected apps in your Dropbox account settings.
  • Your notes remain in your Dropbox account until you delete them there. Taisce holds no copy.

Security

The app is served over HTTPS. Authorisation uses OAuth 2 with PKCE, so no client secret exists in the app or in this repository. Everything rendered from a note is sanitised before it reaches the page. The token in localStorage is the deliberate trade for having no backend; that is why the app loads no third-party scripts.

Children

Taisce is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it. Questions or problems: open an issue at github.com/ptaylor/taisce.

How Dropbox handles your data is described in the Dropbox privacy policy.
