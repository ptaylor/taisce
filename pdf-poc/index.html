What it does

  • Headings, paragraphs, bold and italic, inline and fenced code.
  • Bullet, numbered and task lists; blockquotes and horizontal rules.
  • Links become clickable PDF annotations; simple tables become rows.
  • Images become their alt text rather than a bitmap.

It writes PDF objects directly, using the fonts every reader already has. No canvas, no screenshot, no PDF library — what you drop is what gets measured and laid out once.
